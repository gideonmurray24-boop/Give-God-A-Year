Give God a Year

Give God a Year is more than a book—it’s an invitation. A life-changing challenge to surrender fully, trust boldly, and step into a faith-filled journey like never before.

It is a call to stop living by fear and start living by faith, to release control and trust God with the next step, the next season, and the next year.

For anyone who wants a deeper relationship with God, a clearer sense of purpose, and a fresh start, this book offers a practical, honest, and hopeful path forward.
